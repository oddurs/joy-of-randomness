# Part 2: Movement

## Random Walks and Wandering Through Probability Space

Once we understand what randomness looks like, we study what happens when randomness *accumulates*. A random walk—where each step is random but depends on where you are now—is one of the most powerful models in probability and science. 

In Part 2, we'll follow particles, drunk walkers, and random wanderers through space and time. You'll see how random walks model stock prices, DNA diffusion, animal migrations, and disease spread. The surprising insight: even with no external force pushing you in any particular direction, randomness alone creates structure and patterns.
