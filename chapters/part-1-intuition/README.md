# Part 1: Intuition

## Why Things Look Random (And Why We're Bad at Recognizing It)

Before diving into probability and simulation, we need to understand how randomness *looks*—and how our brains systematically misinterpret it. This part builds intuition for what randomness really is by exploring the mismatch between mathematical randomness and human perception.

We'll see why our pattern-seeking minds find patterns in coin flips, how the law of large numbers actually works (not how we think it works), and what happens when we watch something random unfold over time. These chapters lay the conceptual groundwork for everything that follows.
